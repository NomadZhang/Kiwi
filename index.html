About Us

启未教育（Keyway Education）成立于新加坡，专注于6–16岁学生的英语能力构建与国际学术发展。我们深度理解中国家庭背景与新加坡教育体系的差异，致力于帮助新移民家庭的孩子实现平稳过渡与学术跃升。 Founded in Singapore, Keyway Education specializes in English proficiency and international academic development for students aged 6-16. We deeply understand the transition needs of new immigrant families and bridge the gap to Singapore's education system.
